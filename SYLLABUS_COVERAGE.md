# ABRSM syllabus coverage

ABRSM's current Music Theory information lists the syllabus introduced in 2020. Both the supplied September 2023 link and an official copy hosted under January 2024 describe the same requirements. The April 2023 Qualification Specification, version 1.2, retains that syllabus and gives the detailed examination content for Grades 1 to 5 on pages 16 to 18. Pages 19 to 21 concern higher grades. The official practice resources page also lists 2025 practice papers and model answers as well as the free sample and online practice examinations. Source check on 24 November 2026. [ABRSM US Music Theory](https://www.abrsm.org/en-us/music-theory/about-music-theory) · [Syllabus outline](https://www.abrsm.org/sites/default/files/2023-09/music-theory-syllabus-outline-grades-1-5-from-2020.pdf#page=1) · [January 2024 hosted outline](https://www.abrsm.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/music-theory-syllabus-outline-grades-1-5-from-2020.pdf#page=1) · [Qualification Specification](https://www.abrsm.org/sites/default/files/2023-09/Music%20Theory%20Qual%20Spec%20April%202023%20%5B2023%20rebrand%5D.pdf) · [Practice resources](https://www.abrsm.org/en-gb/practice-resources)

Grades are cumulative. Each row records the grade at which the skill or expansion first applies. References use the printed page numbers in the Qualification Specification. The final column tracks validation of the lesson and assessment. All rows have been mapped to the official scope. Automated validation indicates that tests pass. Independent editorial review is pending. The assessment patterns are TheoryLab designs with original content.

Each mapped lesson ends with three original questions. Automated checks cover question identity, variations and scoring across all 105 objectives. Depending on the skill, a learner selects an option, enters a number, places a note or selects a chord from a list. TheoryLab provides feedback after each answer in lesson checks.
