# Curriculum

Use the cumulative Grades 1 to 5 scope in the official qualification specification. Each registered objective has one introductory grade, one domain, prerequisite IDs, vocabulary, source pages and a bounded assessment strategy. Refer to the [syllabus coverage matrix](SYLLABUS_COVERAGE.md) for the complete objective inventory.

In the domain view, worked examples may connect related ideas across grades. The grade view shows when each requirement is introduced. The diagnostic samples abilities before recommending a route through prerequisite objectives. A practical piano qualification helps with familiarity and does not count towards theory mastery.

Intervals form the first complete lesson sequence. Begin with inclusive letter counting, then use semitone distance to classify quality. Progress through diatonic intervals in the keys set for Grades 3 and 4, then all simple and compound intervals in the Grade 5 scope. Use staff notation and keyboard sound to connect the two independent measurements.

Audio, the circle of fifths as a navigation aid and further harmonic functions can support learning. See the coverage matrix for exam scope. Only validated exam objectives enter the mock blueprint.

Source checked 24 November 2026. [ABRSM Qualification Specification, pp. 12 to 18](https://www.abrsm.org/sites/default/files/2023-09/Music%20Theory%20Qual%20Spec%20April%202023%20%5B2023%20rebrand%5D.pdf).
