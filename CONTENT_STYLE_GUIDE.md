# Content style guide

Use British music vocabulary throughout objectives, notation labels and assessment. Show American equivalents in the glossary as an aid. Keep stems and options in English. Mark Simplified Chinese text with its language for assistive technology.

Lessons start with a concise English definition. In Chinese, connect the notation or sound to a rule, walk through an original worked example, explain a common mistake and include a retrieval cue. End with an English knowledge check. Use full width Chinese punctuation in the explanations.

Every answer explanation should name the underlying decision and explain the distractors. For intervals, count letters inclusively to obtain the number and count semitones to obtain quality. An English vocabulary prompt should explain the instruction without solving the question.

Keep exam active screens in English and withhold feedback until submission. Use only original notation excerpts. Link to ABRSM's sample paper and model answers for independent checks.
