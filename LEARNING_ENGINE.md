# Learning engine

Track lesson visits, attempt history and review schedules per objective. The default provisional threshold requires at least 10 distinct independent questions, 90% accuracy across the latest independent attempt at each distinct item and evidence from multiple variations. Assisted attempts remain visible alongside independent results. Mastery also requires successful delayed retrieval. Allow the learner to configure the thresholds on this device.

After a mistake, assign a fresh question on the same objective. Use review delays of 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. An independent correct answer advances the interval, while an incorrect answer returns it to the first step. A missed review makes the objective due again. The mistake notebook retains the original question, feedback and attempt metadata. Optional learner classifications remain editable.

The daily queue puts due review first, then an available prerequisite or the lowest performing objective. Show visits separately from mastery. Vocabulary attempts have their own identifiers, so they cannot inflate objective accuracy. Use the learner's local calendar for streaks.
